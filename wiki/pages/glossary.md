# Glossary

Domain terms used across the wiki. Link here with `[[glossary]]` when a term first appears.

- **Backwardation** — futures curve where near-term prices are *above* far-dated; signals tight
  supply now. Often a *buy/hold* tilt. Opposite of contango.
- **Contango** — far-dated futures *above* spot; signals ample near-term supply or cost-of-carry.
  Often supports *wait*.
- **CBAM** — EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism; import carbon cost, relevant to [[aluminium]].
- **COT report** — CFTC Commitments of Traders; weekly speculative positioning. Leading
  [[market-signals|signal]].
- **Hedge** — locking a price via futures/forwards without taking physical delivery now. One of
  the four [[recommendation-framework]] actions.
- **ICIS LOR** — ICIS price reference used in petrochemicals; relevant to [[pet]].
- **LME** — London Metal Exchange; benchmark for [[aluminium]] price and warehouse stocks.
- **Malting barley** — barley grade used for brewing; the [[barley]] input that matters to Damm.
- **MEG** — monoethylene glycol; a [[pet]] feedstock alongside PTA.
- **OMIP** — Iberian energy derivatives exchange; power futures for [[energy]].
- **PTA** — purified terephthalic acid; primary [[pet]] feedstock, derived from crude/paraxylene.
- **rPET / vPET** — recycled vs. virgin PET; EU recycled-content rules shift demand between them.
- **Risk/opportunity score** — 0–100 read of how stretched upside-vs-downside is; drives whether
  to act or [[recommendation-framework|monitor]].
- **TTF** — Title Transfer Facility; the European natural-gas benchmark hub → [[energy]].
